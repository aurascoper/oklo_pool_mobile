The three declared choices of v1 stand as declared, with this evidence attached to them. For equilibrium linear sorption of U(VI) only, local equilibrium is supported as a candidate under specific conditions (Bai et al. 2009, abstract), and linear sorption up to 1 to 2 µmol/g in two Fe(III) oxide-bearing materials (Roden et al. 2004, p. 5), with known limits: strong pH dependence (p. 5) and kinetic control in one column at pH 4 with phosphate and high flow (p. 6); the v1 K_d sweep already presents its values as sensitivity bounds at an assumed pH of 7. Reduction of dissolved U(VI) only is questioned, though not settled, by Roden et al. 2004, and the choice is consequential. Under v1 the reaction acts on θC alone, so the fraction of U(VI) removed in one transit of the organic zone does not depend on R (v1 section 5). If sorbed U(VI) were reduced at the same rate, the exponent of that fraction would scale with R, which spans nearly seven orders of magnitude in the sweep (1 to 9,460,501). A version that reduces sorbed U(VI) needs its own amendment, and no estimate of its effect is made here. Finally, the single effective immobilization step is consistent with keeping adsorbed and precipitated uranium apart (Satpathy et al. 2023, abstract), but no source read gives a rate.

The reaction loop pasted on 2026-10-18 from another agent is not adopted. Its sink, ρ_b(1 - φ) k_red K_d C, multiplies a kinetic rate by K_d, an equilibrium partitioning ratio. The units cancel if K_d is in m3/kg (the loop states L/kg, which needs a factor of 1e-3), but no mechanism is defined, and the EPA report warns that its maximum K_d curve may be biased high, partly because some measurements may include precipitation (page 5.75, as quoted in v1 section 4). It calls ρ_b a bulk density and multiplies it by (1 - φ), which counts the solid fraction twice unless ρ_b is a grain density. It uses K_d without a sorbed storage term. Its own update removes reaction_flux dt per bulk volume from the water and adds reaction_flux dt to S, while its conservation check sums φC + (1 - φ)ρ_b S. That sum changes by reaction_flux dt ((1 - φ)ρ_b - 1) each step, so the update conserves it only if (1 - φ)ρ_b = 1. Its zone switch of K_d from 0.1 to 1e5 L/kg, and K_d = f_oc K_oc with a pH-dependent K_oc, have no uranium-specific support in any source read here. The same report asserts a frozen specification with a SHA-256 value and a clean Biofilms working tree. No corresponding file was found on this machine in a search on 2026-10-18 of ~/.gemini/antigravity, ~/Developer/Biofilms, ~/Developer/rna_biofilm_toy and ~/Developer/notes, so that value is not a receipt. v1 already avoids each of these faults: K_d enters only the storage term through R, ρ_b is the dry bulk density (1 - θ) times 2650 kg/m3, S is per kilogram of solid, and the ledger includes the boundary fluxes.
